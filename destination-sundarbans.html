Sundarbans Mangrove Safari

Three days on the water in the world's largest mangrove forest, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Overview

The Sundarbans stretch across the delta of the Padma, Meghna and Brahmaputra rivers. On this tour you live on a comfortable tour boat, cruising wide rivers by day and narrow creeks by country boat at dawn. With an experienced forest guide and an armed forest guard, you will walk forest trails, climb watchtowers and look for spotted deer, monkeys, crocodiles, kingfishers and — if you are very lucky — tiger footprints.

Overnight AC bus travel between Dhaka and Khulna is included before Day 1 and after Day 3.

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 — Khulna to Harbaria

    Morning pickup in Khulna and transfer to Mongla. Board the tour boat, meet your guide and cruise down the Pashur River. Afternoon walk on the Harbaria eco-trail and watchtower, then dinner on deck.

  2. Day 2 — Kotka and Jamtola

    Sunrise creek trip by country boat to watch birds and deer. Walk to the Kotka watchtower and through the forest to Jamtola sea beach. Evening barbecue on board under the stars.

  3. Day 3 — Karamjal and return

    Visit the Karamjal wildlife centre, home to crocodile and deer breeding programmes. Lunch on board while sailing back to Mongla, then transfer to Khulna for the evening bus to Dhaka.

What's included
