Walk among the clouds in the highest hills of Bangladesh.

Overview

Bandarban is a land of green hills, waterfalls and indigenous communities such as the Marma and Bawm. You stay at a hill resort, ride the famous open-top local jeeps (chander gari) up winding roads, and take easy guided treks to viewpoints that often sit above the clouds.

Overnight AC bus travel between Dhaka and Bandarban is included before Day 1 and after Day 4. Treks are easy to moderate; comfortable shoes are recommended.

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 — Arrival and Nilachal

    Arrive in the morning and check in to the hill resort. Afternoon visit to Meghla lake and its hanging bridge, then sunset over the hills from Nilachal.

  2. Day 2 — Nilgiri and Shoilo Propat

    Ride a local jeep up to Nilgiri, one of the highest viewpoints in the country. Stop at the Shoilo Propat waterfall and a Bawm village on the way back.

  3. Day 3 — Golden Temple and Marma culture

    Visit the Buddha Dhatu Jadi (Golden Temple), then enjoy a traditional Marma lunch and an easy guided walk through hill villages.

  4. Day 4 — Free morning and return

    Relax at the resort or explore the town market. Evening AC bus back to Dhaka.
