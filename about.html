For the love of going places.

Meet the small Dhaka team that plans, guides and looks after every GhuriFiri tour.

OUR MISSION

Make travelling in Bangladesh simple, safe and affordable.

We want every traveller — first-timers, families and student groups — to discover the beauty of their own country without stress. That means honest prices per person, trusted local guides, and hotels and transport we have checked ourselves.

We also care about the places we visit: we keep groups small, follow forest rules in the Sundarbans and work with local families in the hills.

Our history

  1. 2018 — A Facebook page and a phone

    Two friends from Dhaka began organising weekend trips to Cox's Bazar, sharing them on social media and taking bookings by phone.

  2. 2020 — Into the Sundarbans

    We partnered with a licensed boat operator in Mongla and ran our first three-day mangrove safari.

  3. 2023 — Sylhet and Bandarban

    Tea garden walks in Sylhet and hill treks in Bandarban joined our list, guided by people who live there.

  4. 2026 — Booking online

    This website lets travellers compare packages, see prices and send booking requests at any time of day.
