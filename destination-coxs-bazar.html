Cox's Bazar Beach Escape

Sun, sea and seafood on the world's longest natural sea beach.

Overview

Cox's Bazar's golden beach runs unbroken for more than 120 km along the Bay of Bengal. You stay in a sea-view hotel a short walk from the sand, drive the scenic Marine Drive between hills and sea, and have plenty of free time to swim, relax and enjoy fresh seafood.

Overnight AC bus travel between Dhaka and Cox's Bazar is included before Day 1 and after Day 3.

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 — Arrival and Laboni beach

    Arrive in the morning and check in to your sea-view hotel. Relax on Laboni beach, then watch the sunset from Sugandha point with your guide.

  2. Day 2 — Marine Drive, Himchari and Inani

    Drive the Marine Drive coastal road to Himchari waterfall and hill viewpoint, then on to the coral-stone Inani beach. Evening visit to the Burmese market.

  3. Day 3 — Free morning and return

    Enjoy a sunrise walk or a last swim. Check out after lunch and board the evening AC bus back to Dhaka.
