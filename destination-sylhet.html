Sylhet Tea Garden Trail

Tea estates, a swamp forest and crystal-clear rivers in Bangladesh's green north-east.

Trip at a glance

  • Region Sylhet division
  • Tour type Nature
  • Duration 3 days / 2 nights
  • Starts from Dhaka (overnight AC bus)

Overview

Sylhet is the tea capital of Bangladesh. You stay in a cottage at a tea resort, walk between neatly trimmed tea bushes, glide by boat through the flooded Ratargul swamp forest and watch the stone-collecting boats on the clear Piyain river at Jaflong.

Overnight AC bus travel between Dhaka and Sylhet is included before Day 1 and after Day 3.

Day-by-day itinerary

  1. Day 1 — Tea gardens

    Arrive in the morning and check in to the tea resort. Afternoon walk through the Lakkatura and Malnichhara tea estates, then visit the Hazrat Shahjalal shrine.

  2. Day 2 — Ratargul swamp forest

    Ride a small wooden boat through Ratargul, Bangladesh's only freshwater swamp forest (water levels permitting), then relax back at the resort.

  3. Day 3 — Jaflong and return

    Drive to Jaflong to see the Piyain river and the hills of Meghalaya, and visit a Khasia village. Evening AC bus back to Dhaka.

What's included

  • Two nights in a tea resort cottage
  • Breakfast and dinner
  • Local transport by private car
  • Local tour guide
  • Boat hire and entry fees
